Remindly – Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last Updated: November 2026

Privacy Policy

Remindly ("App", Package ID: com.vexlabs.remindly), developed by VexLabs ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), is built on a strict Privacy-First, Local-First architecture. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled, stored, and protected when you use our application.

1. Zero-Server & 100% Local Data Storage Architecture

  • All user-generated content—including reminders, tasks, calendar events, notes, appointments, bill and subscription amounts, payment schedules, custom categories, and local profile preferences (such as display name and avatar image selected from your device)—is stored exclusively on your device's internal SQLite database and local application sandbox.
  • VexLabs operates NO external backend servers, cloud databases, or telemetry pipelines that collect, read, or store your personal reminders or financial records. We cannot view, access, or recover your local data.

2. No Data Collection, No Ads & No Third-Party Sharing

  • Your personal data and financial reminder records are NEVER sold, rented, monetized, traded, or disclosed to advertising networks, data brokers, analytics providers, or any third parties.
  • Remindly contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking scripts, or cross-app user profiling tools.

3. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

  • Remindly offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature using Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API.
  • If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, the App requests access strictly to the restricted drive.appdata (appDataFolder) scope and basic account profile information (email address, display name, and profile picture URL) solely to display your connected account status inside the App.
  • How Backup Data is Stored: Your backup file is uploaded directly from your device to your personal Google Drive's hidden appDataFolder. Neither VexLabs nor any third party can access, read, or modify files inside your Google Drive.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Compliance): Remindly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used exclusively to provide user-facing backup and restore functionality and is never used for advertising, AI/ML model training, or shared with third parties.

4. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly to display scheduled reminder and bill alerts locally on your device.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Ensures time-sensitive reminders trigger at the exact minute configured by you.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Automatically reschedules your active local reminders after your device restarts.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Optional permission requested so the operating system does not delay or suppress critical notifications in deep sleep mode.
  • Photos / Media Picker: Used only when you voluntarily choose a local profile picture; the selected image remains on your device.

5. Data Retention, User Control & Complete Data Deletion

  • Local Data Deletion: You can edit or delete any reminder at any time within the App. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling Remindly from your device permanently and immediately erases all locally stored SQLite databases, preferences, and profile data.
  • Cloud Backup & Account Deletion: You can sign out, disconnect your Google Account, or manage your backup directly from Settings > Backup inside the App, or revoke access and delete stored app data at any time via your Google Account Security Settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and Google Drive Settings (Settings > Manage Apps > Remindly).

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Remindly does not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Because all data is stored locally on your device and optionally in your own Google Drive account, you retain 100% ownership, possession, access, portability (JSON export/backup), and deletion control over your data at all times.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical or regulatory changes. The latest version is always accessible within the App under Settings > About > Privacy Policy and on this page. For any privacy inquiries, please contact VexLabs via the support email listed on our official Google Play Store developer page.
